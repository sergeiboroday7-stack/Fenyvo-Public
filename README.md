## Messenger Without Phone Number or Email

**FENYVO** is a messenger for Android and Windows.

Create an account using just a **username and password**. No phone number or email address is required.

Stay connected with friends, family, and communities through private messaging, group chats, file sharing, and voice and video calls.

## Features

- **No phone number required** — register with a username and password.
- **No email required** — create an account without an email address.
- **Private chats** — communicate directly with other users.
- **Group chats** — stay connected with multiple participants.
- **Voice messages** — communicate using audio recordings.
- **File sharing** — send files and media.
- **Voice calls** — talk with other users.
- **Video calls** — communicate face to face.
- **Android and Windows** — use FENYVO on supported platforms.

## Privacy

FENYVO is designed to offer an alternative to messengers that require a phone number or email address during registration.

A username and password are sufficient to create an account.

Learn more in the [Privacy Policy](https://fenyvo.com/privacy.html) and [Terms of Use](https://fenyvo.com/terms.html).
